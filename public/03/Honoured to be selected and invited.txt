Honoured to be selected and invited to attend “The Road to $5 Billion” Validation Workshop as a founder. An important national dialogue focused on shaping Sri Lanka’s IT industry assessment and strategic roadmap towards achieving US$5 billion in digital / IT exports by 2030.
I was proud to be there representing not only my own journey as a founder, but also the IT firms, startups, entrepreneurs, and technology ecosystem of the North.
Being in the same room with respected leaders from the Ministry of Digital Economy, GovTech Sri Lanka, World Bank Group / IFC, KPMG, and the wider technology industry was a strong reminder: Sri Lanka does not lack talent.
What we need is alignment, execution, policy support, investment access, and one serious national push.
It was also a privilege to connect with inspiring leaders including Dr. Hans Wijayasuriya, Eranga Weeraratne, Chathuranga Abeysinghe and Bhanuka Harischandra, whose work and thinking continue to shape Sri Lanka’s digital economy and innovation direction.
I see this as a responsibility to bring more visibility, opportunity, and collaboration to the Northern ICT and startup ecosystem. Let’s take Northern technology from local potential to global relevance. The road to $5 billion must include every region of Sri Lanka and I strongly believe that the North is ready to contribute.
