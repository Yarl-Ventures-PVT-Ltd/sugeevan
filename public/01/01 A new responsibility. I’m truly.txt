01 A new responsibility. I’m truly honoured to be appointed as the Chairman of the NCIT -  Northern Chamber of Information Technology for the 2026–2027 term.
First, my sincere thanks to the NCIT members for the trust and confidence placed in me and the newly elected Board. My congratulations and best wishes to all fellow Board Members and Office Bearers. 
The North has talent, companies, students, freelancers, innovators, educators, and entrepreneurs with real potential. Now, what we need is stronger collaboration, better industry visibility, meaningful partnerships, investor access, talent development, and one united voice to represent the Northern ICT ecosystem.
Through NCIT, our goal is clear: To build a stronger, connected, and future-ready ICT industry from the North. One that creates opportunities locally and competes globally.
I warmly invite all IT companies, startups, freelancers, professionals, training institutes, students, investors, diaspora leaders, Investors, development partners, and government stakeholders to join this mission.
This is the time to come together. Not as separate efforts, but as one ecosystem.
Let’s shape the future of Northern ICT together.
