Meet Sugeevan Vsg, Founder & CEO of MediMan - a digital healthcare platform fully designed, engineered, and built here in Sri Lanka, with the vision of becoming the country’s first globally scaling telehealth super-app.
MediMan connects patients with trusted doctors anytime, anywhere - both locally and internationally. With the rising healthcare access challenges, especially for rural communities and Sri Lankan families living abroad, this is already proving to be a life-changing solution.
Coming from Jaffna, with no major background or influence, Sugeevan invested his entire life’s savings, time, and every bit of skill he had to build something meaningful for our people. Today, MediMan has grown into a platform far beyond what anyone expected could emerge from the North - and now stands on the edge of becoming a future Sri Lankan unicorn with global expansion potential.
MediMan is more than an app - it’s a national mission to connect every Sri Lankan with the right doctor at the right time, without barriers.
